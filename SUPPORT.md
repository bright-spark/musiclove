## Content and copyright

theradio.fm aggregates and links to **publicly available** streams and pages. It does not host, copy, or modify third-party media files. Rights remain with the original owners and platforms.

### Takedown requests

Rights holders who believe a link or embed is inappropriate may request review. We consider good-faith requests promptly.

1. Email **studio@theradio.fm** with:
   * Your name and organization (if any)
   * The URL(s) on theradio.fm (or in this repo) at issue
   * Description of the content and your rights in it
   * Preferred resolution (e.g. remove link, replace source)
2. Optionally also reach Martin Myburgh directly as noted in the README.

We may remove or adjust links while we review. This process does not waive any legal rights of either party.

## Resetting the app experience

Returning visitors who prefer the full app are redirected to `/app` via a preference cookie. To return to the marketing landing page:

https://theradio.fm/reset

## Status

There is no separate status page. If theradio.fm is unreachable, check your network and [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflarestatus.com/) status; then open an issue if the outage appears specific to this project.
